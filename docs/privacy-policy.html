Politica de Confidentialitate

Chestionare Barca — Ultima actualizare: 29 martie 2026

1. Introducere

Aceasta politica de confidentialitate descrie modul in care aplicatia Chestionare Barca colecteaza, utilizeaza si protejeaza datele dumneavoastra. Aplicatia este un instrument de pregatire pentru examenul CAA (Clasa C si D) si nu necesita crearea unui cont de utilizator sau autentificare.

2. Date stocate local pe dispozitiv

Aplicatia stocheaza urmatoarele date exclusiv pe dispozitivul dumneavoastra, folosind AsyncStorage:

  • Istoricul chestionarelor completate (raspunsuri, scoruri, date)
  • Setarile aplicatiei (tema, preferinte notificari)
  • Realizarile obtinute (badge-uri)
  • Streak-ul zilnic (numarul de zile consecutive de utilizare)

Aceste date nu parasesc dispozitivul dumneavoastra si nu sunt transmise catre servere externe, cu exceptia cazurilor descrise mai jos.

3. Supabase (stocarea datelor pe server)

Aplicatia utilizeaza serviciul Supabase pentru urmatoarele functionalitati:

  • Identificator de dispozitiv: Un ID unic de dispozitiv este generat si transmis catre Supabase. Acesta nu contine informatii personale identificabile.
  • Rascumparare vouchere: Datele privind rascumpararea codurilor promotionale (voucher) sunt stocate pe serverele Supabase.

Nu sunt colectate sau stocate informatii personale (nume, email, numar de telefon) prin Supabase.

4. Google AdMob (publicitate)

Aplicatia afiseaza reclame prin Google AdMob. AdMob poate colecta:

  • Identificatori de dispozitiv (Advertising ID)
  • Date despre interactiunile cu reclamele
  • Informatii tehnice despre dispozitiv (model, versiune sistem de operare)

Colectarea acestor date este gestionata de Google conform Politicii de Confidentialitate Google. La prima utilizare, aplicatia va solicita consimtamantul dumneavoastra pentru personalizarea reclamelor, conform reglementarilor GDPR.

Puteti modifica preferintele privind reclamele oricand din sectiunea Setari a aplicatiei.

5. Notificari

Aplicatia utilizeaza exclusiv notificari locale (programate pe dispozitiv) pentru remindere privind streak-ul zilnic. Nu exista un server de notificari push si nicio data nu este transmisa catre servere externe in scopul notificarilor.

6. Drepturile dumneavoastra (GDPR)

In conformitate cu Regulamentul General privind Protectia Datelor (GDPR), aveti urmatoarele drepturi:

  • Dreptul de acces: Puteti solicita informatii despre datele colectate.
  • Dreptul la stergere: Dezinstalarea aplicatiei sterge toate datele locale. Pentru datele de pe Supabase, contactati-ne la adresa de mai jos.
  • Dreptul la opozitie: Puteti refuza personalizarea reclamelor prin formularul de consimtamant UMP sau din Setari.
  • Dreptul la portabilitate: Datele locale sunt stocate pe dispozitivul dumneavoastra si pot fi sterse prin dezinstalarea aplicatiei.

7. Securitatea datelor

Datele locale sunt protejate de mecanismele de securitate ale sistemului de operare al dispozitivului. Comunicarea cu Supabase se realizeaza prin conexiuni criptate (HTTPS).

8. Modificari ale politicii

Ne rezervam dreptul de a actualiza aceasta politica. Orice modificare va fi publicata pe aceasta pagina cu data actualizarii.

9. Contact

Pentru intrebari sau solicitari privind confidentialitatea datelor:

Privacy Policy

Chestionare Barca — Last updated: March 29, 2026

1. Introduction

This privacy policy describes how the Chestionare Barca application collects, uses, and protects your data. The app is a study tool for the CAA exam (Class C and D) and does not require user account creation or authentication.

2. Data stored locally on device

The app stores the following data exclusively on your device using AsyncStorage:

  • Quiz history (answers, scores, dates)
  • App settings (theme, notification preferences)
  • Achievements (badges)
  • Daily streak (consecutive days of usage)

This data does not leave your device and is not transmitted to external servers, except as described below.

3. Supabase (server-side data storage)

The app uses the Supabase service for the following functionality:

  • Device identifier: A unique device ID is generated and sent to Supabase. It does not contain personally identifiable information.
  • Voucher redemption: Data related to promotional code (voucher) redemption is stored on Supabase servers.

No personal information (name, email, phone number) is collected or stored through Supabase.

4. Google AdMob (advertising)

The app displays advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID)
  • Ad interaction data
  • Technical device information (model, OS version)

Data collection is managed by Google according to the Google Privacy Policy. On first use, the app will request your consent for ad personalization in compliance with GDPR regulations.

You can change your ad preferences at any time from the Settings section of the app.

5. Notifications

The app uses only local notifications (scheduled on device) for daily streak reminders. There is no push notification server and no data is transmitted to external servers for notification purposes.

6. Your rights (GDPR)

In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the following rights:

  • Right of access: You may request information about collected data.
  • Right to erasure: Uninstalling the app deletes all local data. For data on Supabase, contact us at the address below.
  • Right to object: You may refuse ad personalization via the UMP consent form or from Settings.
  • Right to portability: Local data is stored on your device and can be deleted by uninstalling the app.

7. Data security

Local data is protected by the device operating system's security mechanisms. Communication with Supabase is conducted through encrypted connections (HTTPS).

8. Changes to this policy

We reserve the right to update this policy. Any changes will be published on this page with the updated date.

9. Contact

For questions or requests regarding data privacy:

Email: popescut94@gmail.com
